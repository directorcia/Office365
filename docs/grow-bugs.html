01 / EXPLORE

A repeatable beginning

Leave starting counts blank for random amounts. A seed and the same settings recreate the same world.

02 / SURVIVE

Find a little food

A bug stays green while at least 75% of its time remains, then fades through amber to red. Eating blue food resets its time and color, and it grows a little while keeping its movement traits.

03 / BEGIN AGAIN

Life finds a way

Two green bugs produce a child when they first touch, once per encounter; newborns must separate before making contact births. Starved bugs are replaced while others survive. Evolution gives children and replacements inherited, mutated traits. The population is capped at 100; food is capped at 300. If all bugs die at once, the simulation ends.

A simple simulation, not a scientific model.
