          Campaign Rules

          These paragraphs describe Classic mode. Manual-based campaigns use separate movement/combat phases, combined attacks, artillery, strategy, local/general supply, fuel and readiness. Germany cannot move on turn 1. See the manual-based guide for that mode and its explicitly documented approximations.

          Command the German offensive over twelve daily turns, 16-27 December. Hold at least eight objective points after the final American response to win. Eliminate all opposing formations for an early victory.

          Select a German counter or a formation in the roster. In Move mode, white dots mark reachable destinations; the yellow outline marks the selected unit. In Attack mode, select an adjacent American counter. A unit can move several times within its allowance but may attack only once. Combat consumes all remaining movement.

          Undo Move reverses movement orders in reverse order, restoring movement points and town control. Successful combat, ending the turn, restarting, importing a save or reloading the page clears undo history.

          Counter numbers show strength / remaining movement. Armor has five movement points; infantry has four. Plains, roads and towns cost one point, forests two, ridges and rivers three. Entering an enemy-adjacent hex ends movement. Units cannot stack.

          Combat odds compare current strength, attack and defense ratings, terrain and supply. Forests, ridges and towns favor defenders. A seeded die roll determines losses and possible retreat. Blocked retreats cause extra losses; zero strength eliminates a formation.

          Supply must trace a route to the friendly map edge, east for Germany and west for America, without crossing enemy units or unoccupied enemy zones of control. Isolated units lose two movement points, fight at reduced effectiveness and lose one strength per day after the first turn.

          Fog covers the opening. Snow can occur on days four through seven, reducing movement and making off-road armor movement more expensive. Clear skies from day eight add one point to American combat damage. American reinforcements arrive on days three, five and seven. Arriving counters appear immediately on the map with a NEW marker for that turn. The reinforcement list below the map lets you locate each arrival.

          End Turn runs the American response and starts the next day. Town control changes as units enter. The browser autosaves after orders; Export Save and Import Save transfer campaigns. On a focused map, use arrow keys and Enter to select a hex. On small screens the map scrolls horizontally.

          This is an original scenario with a simplified ruleset, not the original game's exact map or combat tables.
