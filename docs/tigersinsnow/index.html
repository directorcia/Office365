              Order of Battle

              Unit symbols: [=] armour, [X] infantry, [P] airborne / paratroopers, [M] mechanised infantry / cavalry. These distinguish formation types visually; their existing movement and combat rules are unchanged.

              The two numbers beside each active formation are combat points / remaining movement points. For example, 55 / 0 means 55 combat points (current fighting strength) and no movement points remaining for the current phase.

              Select a formation to inspect it on the map; only your commanded army can receive orders. Reinforcement schedules show arrival turn, date and current status. Scheduled arrivals join after that day's combat; blocked arrivals are delayed.

                    How victory works

                    Campaign Rules

                    New campaigns use the Manual-based rules: separate movement and combat phases, combined attacks, artillery, strategy, supply, fuel and readiness. Germany cannot move on turn 1. New player-aid campaigns use provisional 24 MP infantry / 30 MP mechanized allowances; existing saves retain their allowances. The rules are an adaptation; see the Manual-based guide for details and documented approximations.

                    Most points wins. The Allies are shown with 699 victory points (VP) already in a full campaign, and 324 in the short scenario, because Germany is attacking and must break through. Add that to the points the Allies earn. The higher total is winning, and the board rounds those totals to whole numbers. Classic campaigns show America with 7. Full-campaign levels: Allies 649+, 400–648, 0–399, then Germany 1–250, 251–500, 501+. Short scenario: Allies 274+, 150–273, 0–149, then Germany 1–100, 101–375, 376+. Classic: America 0+, Germany 1+. Each destroyed enemy combat point is worth 1. German units also score Bastogne 50, Marche 25 and Rochefort 25 at the end of a day, plus marked edges (south or west 3, north 10) and exits. Those German place awards double while the computer plays Germany. Allied units score 20 on a marked eastern edge.

                    Command either side across the twelve daily turns from 16–27 December. Follow the victory assessment on the campaign screen for the current objective and scoring. The computer controls the opposing side unless you choose Two players / Hotseat.

                    Use the controls beside the map to issue movement and combat orders. New campaigns autosave in the browser; Export Save and Import Save transfer campaigns. Existing saves retain their saved rules.

                    This is an original scenario, not the original game's exact map or combat tables.

                      Start a New Campaign?

                      Your current autosave will be replaced. New campaigns use the Manual-based rules. Choose your command side; the computer controls the opposing side.

                      Most points wins. The Allies start with 699 victory points (VP) already because Germany is attacking. Add that to the points the Allies earn. The higher total wins. The victory level is how far ahead that total is on the board. The short scenario uses 324. Classic saves show America starting with 7.

                      Player's aid card options

                      Rating 5 uses the card's combat points. Each level above/below 5 adds/subtracts 3 CP per formation, including reinforcements. Imports retain saved ratings without applying them again.

                      Manual campaigns incorporate the player’s aid card’s full roster, reinforcement dates, ratings and cumulative victory scoring on the rebuilt 19 × 11 map, plus the Apple-version supplement’s second combat, 25-CP elimination threshold, weighted strategy pressure, air-interdiction reports and R-key status display. Starting positions and unspecified formulas remain documented adaptations. Computer vs Computer supplies the demo; obsolete Apple disk/monitor setup is replaced by browser controls.
